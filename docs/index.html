Assignments

A1: Holiday Lights

Three independent LED rhythms, current-limiting resistors, and a six-second repeating pattern.

A2: Firefly

A light-up toy that rests with a red glow and wakes into blue and pulsing green while its button is held.

A3: Daylight Monitor

A photoresistor voltage divider that fades green to blue as a room loses light, with a red alert when it gets too dark.
